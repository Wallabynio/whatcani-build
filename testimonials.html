What our clients
actually say.

Specific outcomes, real projects, honest results — from developers, builders and owners across Sydney and NSW.

"

We were three weeks from exchange on a corner site in Earlwood and needed to know whether the R3 zoning would actually carry four townhouses. The feasibility review came back in six business days: a realistic yield of three, not four, a clear read on the Canterbury-Bankstown DCP setbacks, and a DA risk profile our lender could work with. We bought at the right price because of it.

Owner-developer
Townhouse feasibility · Earlwood, Canterbury-Bankstown, 2025
"

Our architect's first scheme for a dual occupancy in Ryde blew the 8.5-metre height control and fell short on landscaped area — and we only found out after paying for it. The pre-design brief reset the envelope against the Ryde LEP before the redesign, and the second scheme went through without a single amended plan. It saved us roughly $15,000 in abortive design fees.

Residential builder
Pre-design brief · Ryde, 2025
"

The Site Check flagged that our Marrickville site sat inside a heritage conservation area — something two agents and our own searches had missed. For $600 we avoided lodging a dual-occupancy DA that Inner West Council would almost certainly have refused, and pivoted to an extension pathway that actually stacked up.

Property investor
Site Check · Marrickville, Inner West, 2025
"

Fifteen years developing in the Sutherland Shire and this was the clearest planning advice I've been given. The report showed the numbers — FSR, minimum frontage, likely conditions — and made a straight call to go CDC rather than DA on our Caringbah duplex. The certificate was issued in 16 business days.

Developer
Feasibility & pathway review · Caringbah, Sutherland Shire, 2025
